### Tool entry (for Section 8)

**Re-Skilling & Course Guide (Kompetenslyft)**

Why: When the honest answer is that a gap stays a gap, show the person the path to close it, without pushing them onto a course they may not need.

- Triggered by the honest outcome. Application Check and Match Analysis already name the requirements a person cannot yet meet. When a gap cannot be covered by reframing existing experience, this is where that dead end becomes an action path
- Recommends the few courses that actually close the decisive gap for the target role, driven by what the labour market is asking for, not by browsing a full catalogue
- Always shows the fork. Apply now anyway, faster but a lower fit, set beside retrain, slower but a higher fit. The person sees the trade-off and decides. Retraining is one option, never the default
- Checks first whether prior learning can be recognised. Sometimes the skill is already there and only needs to be validated, which can shorten or remove the course entirely, so the person is not sent to study something they have effectively already done
- Honest about outcomes and timing. Shows the real employment figures for a pathway where they exist, and is plain that training pays off slowly, often over years rather than months, so no one retrains on a false promise
- Works whatever the person's comfort with technology. A light, spoken mode guides the next step out loud, and a coach can pick up the same thread, so the education route is not gated by digital confidence
- Augments the coach, does not replace them. The recommendation and its reasons feed the coach conversation, where the human judgement about whether retraining is right for this life actually happens

### Promoting paragraph (for the Executive Summary or Section 8 intro)

For some people the blocker is not how they present who they are, but a genuine skills gap that has to be closed before the right work is within reach. Education is therefore not a footnote to the job search, but a first-class route back to work, sitting beside applying directly rather than underneath it. It is what makes the 2030 goal read the way it is written, matching people to the right job or education, with both routes given real support instead of one being assumed.

### Honest sourcing note (non-technical, for "How We Build It Responsibly" / Section 7 or a footnote)

A check of the Swedish public data sources found that the course catalogue, the skills classifications, and the employment-outcome figures are available as open public feeds, and that course-demand can be read from public labour-market data, with the caveat that the most authoritative demand list is published as a periodic file rather than a live feed. The one piece not yet available as open data is per-programme recognition of prior learning (validering), so it is flagged as a known gap to source properly rather than guessed at.
