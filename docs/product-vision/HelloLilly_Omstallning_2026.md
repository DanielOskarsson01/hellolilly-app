# HelloLilly - Omställning: Changing Direction in Sweden

**Deep-dive companion to the Jobseeker Product Vision, Version 4 · October 2026 · Confidential draft**

An updated view of omställning, career transition, in Sweden: who is changing and why, what the Swedish system offers, where people fall through it, and how HelloLilly connects a person to every part of it. Replaces the July 2026 "Omställning Area" document. Section 6 describes the tool, the Omställning Guide, and the engine it is built on; each step is described in more detail in the companion **Learn and Re-skill: The Second Route**.

---

## Opening

Omställning used to describe something that happened to a few people at a time: a factory closing, a branch office shutting, an injury that ended a career. In 2026 it describes a large part of the labour market. Work is changing faster than people's training, careers last longer than the jobs inside them, and many people will change direction more than once.

Sweden has built more support for this than almost any country. Since the 2022 reform of the employment protection rules, there is a transition study grant for people mid-career, transition organisations funded by collective agreements, a public transition organisation for those without one, labour-market training through Arbetsförmedlingen, free adult education, vocational colleges, and free study and careers guidance in every municipality.

And yet the support misses the people who need it most. The transition study grant has so far been used mostly by higher-educated people in secure occupations. In 2026, close to half of its budget is forecast to go unused, because too few eligible people apply. The money exists. The routes exist. What is missing is the connection between the person and the route, at the moment it matters, in language they understand, with someone beside them.

That connection is exactly what HelloLilly is built to be.

## 1. Who Is Changing, and Why

There is no typical career changer. The reason for the change shapes everything that follows: the emotional starting point, the financial runway, the right route and the honest expectation.

| Driver | Who it typically hits | What they usually need first |
|---|---|---|
| **Structural change and automation** | Mid-career administrative, factory, warehouse and transport workers; often older, often outside the big cities | A realistic adjacent direction, quickly; an income bridge; early outreach |
| **AI and digital change** | Back-office, data entry, support and administrative roles | Short, practical upskilling into adjacent digital work, or a move into people-facing work |
| **Redundancy and closure** | Whole workforces at once, in declining industries | Rapid placement where possible, re-training where not; the transition organisation's support |
| **Health, injury and ageing** | Physical trades, care and transport workers | The formal routes back to work, matching by physical demand, and psychological support |
| **Foreign qualifications** | Skilled newcomers: nurses, engineers, teachers, pharmacists | Recognition of what they already know, plain-Swedish guidance, a bridge role while it happens |
| **A chosen change** | Anyone mid-career, often between the mid-thirties and mid-forties | Testing the direction before investing; honest numbers |
| **The market has simply moved on** | Long-term jobseekers applying to the same kind of role for a year | A diagnosis: the role, not the application, is the problem |

The evidence adds two things to this picture. Whether a change is chosen or forced shapes how it feels for years afterwards, and money is the most consistent predictor of how a transition ends. So the first question is always **why**, and the second is always **what can you carry**.

## 2. The Swedish System, Simply

The system is rich, but it is spread across many actors, each with its own rules, language and timing. This is the map HelloLilly holds so the person does not have to.

| Actor | What it offers | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **The transition organisations** (such as TRR, TSL and Omställningsfonden, depending on sector and collective agreement) | Advice, coaching, help to find new work, support for study, and help applying for the transition study grant, including a supporting statement | Employees covered by a collective agreement, often while still employed or after redundancy |
| **The public transition organisation** (at Kammarkollegiet) | Guidance on transition and study support | Employees without a collective agreement, and some self-employed people |
| **CSN** | The transition study grant and loan; ordinary study funding | People studying, on the conditions of each grant |
| **Arbetsförmedlingen** | Registration, matching services through providers like HelloLilly, labour-market training, work placements and subsidised employment | Registered jobseekers |
| **Försäkringskassan** | Sickness benefit, the rehabilitation chain, and since March 2026 a way to test work at one's own employer while on full sickness benefit | People on sick leave |
| **The municipality** | Adult education (komvux), Swedish for immigrants, the recruitment grant for people with short education, and free, independent study and careers guidance | Residents |
| **Vocational colleges, folk high schools and universities** | The courses themselves, many of them tuition-free | Students who meet each programme's requirements |
| **Validation bodies** | Recognition of prior learning and of foreign qualifications | Anyone with skills learned outside formal education or abroad |

**Where it breaks.** Each actor works well inside its own lines. The person, though, lives across all of them. Nobody joins them up for someone who does not know they exist, does not understand the official language, or does not have the energy to ask six organisations the same question.

## 3. The Transition Study Grant, as It Stands in October 2026

The strongest single instrument in the system, and the one that most clearly shows both the promise and the gap. Figures from CSN, 2026, checked in October 2026; they are updated every year.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | From the year a person turns 27 to the year they turn 63. From the year they turn 62, at most 10 weeks. The loan part is available until the year they turn 60. |
| **Work condition** | Worked at least 8 of the last 14 years, and at least 12 of the last 24 months |
| **Collective agreement** | Not required |
| **What it pays** | 80 per cent of previous income, at most 5,773 kronor a week before tax in 2026; a loan of up to 3,540 kronor a week can be added |
| **How long** | Up to 44 weeks full-time, or longer part-time (for example 88 weeks at half time) |
| **The condition** | The education must strengthen the person's position in the labour market |
| **When to apply** | From 1 October, for studies starting between January and June the following year. From 1 April, for studies starting between July and December. Applications sent too early are rejected. |
| **The budget** | Limited per application round, split one third for public-sector employees and two thirds for private-sector employees. When the money in a round runs out, remaining applications are rejected, so the date of application matters. |

**The paradox of 2026.** Two facts sit side by side. The budget is first come, first served within each round, which rewards whoever applies fastest and most completely, usually the already advantaged. And yet, across the year, close to half the budget is forecast to go unused: about 3.2 of 7.3 billion kronor paid out, because too few eligible people apply. Both point to the same gap. The people who would gain most do not know the grant exists, do not believe it is for them, or are beaten by the form.

**What that means for HelloLilly.** The grant does not go to the most deserving. It goes to the most prepared. HelloLilly's job is to make the people the system currently misses the best prepared people in each round: aware, eligible-checked, direction-tested, with a complete application and a coach-reviewed motivation ready when the window opens.

### When the grant is not the answer

Many long-term jobseekers do not meet the work condition, because a long gap breaks the twelve-months rule. That is a different door, not a dead end:

- **Labour-market training** through Arbetsförmedlingen: free, short, aimed at occupations with shortages, with activity support. The evidence favours exactly this shape.
- **The recruitment grant (studiestartsstöd)**, for people with short previous education and a long time out of work, reached through the municipality.
- **Ordinary study funding** from CSN, open more widely, without the queue.
- **Work testing on sick leave**, from March 2026, for people on full sickness benefit, at their own employer, for limited periods.
- **Free study and careers guidance** in every municipality, independent of any course provider.

## 5. HelloLilly's Role in the System

HelloLilly does not replace any actor in the Swedish system. It connects them around one person.

- **It is the front door.** One place where a jobseeker learns what exists for them, in plain Swedish, instead of six websites and six logins.
- **It is the preparer.** Agents gather the facts, check eligibility, draft the motivation, find the courses and assemble the application, so the person arrives at each actor ready.
- **It is the connector.** It points to the transition organisation, the municipality's free guidance, Arbetsförmedlingen's training and Försäkringskassan's routes by name, at the moment they are relevant, and never pretends to be them.
- **It keeps the fork open.** Applying now, a placement and re-training are always shown side by side.
- **It keeps the human in the loop.** The coach signs off any long education and carries the motivation the evidence says no tool can.
- **It learns.** Which routes work for which people, recorded as dated facts, never as proof of cause.

For HelloLilly as an organisation, this matters directly. Its goal is to match 10,000 people to the right job **or education** by 2030, on results-based funding. Education is half the promise, and the transition system is where much of that half is won or lost.

## 6. The Tool: From Plan to Product

Omställning in HelloLilly is not a separate product and not a page of information. It is one guided flow, the **Omställning Guide (Omställningsguiden)**, built around one engine that already has a plan: the education-finding engine at the heart of the **Education Matcher**. The guide is the journey; the engine is what makes each step concrete.

### The guided flow

| Step | Tool | What the person gets | Engine underneath |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Why are you here?** | Transition Compass | The driver, the financial runway, digital comfort, confidence; the routes back from sick leave | Plain-Swedish conversation, spoken if preferred |
| **2. Where could you go?** | Direction Explorer | Three or four realistic adjacent directions, each with demand, adverts and salary | The national map of occupations and skills, matched to the fact bank |
| **3. What do you already have?** | Prior Learning Check | What could be recognised, including foreign qualifications | Taxonomy-backed guidance; the coach for the contact |
| **4. What closes the gap?** | Education Matcher | The few courses that close it, free ones and short ones first, with placements | **The course-fit engine** (below) |
| **5. Can you afford it?** | Funding Navigator and Transition Economics | Eligibility, dated facts, the application pack, months and money side by side | Funding rules file, checked yearly |
| **6. Try it first** | Test Before You Invest | An informational talk, a shadow day, a taster, a work trial | Contact Planner, Placement Finder, coach network |
| **7. Decide** | Transition View | The coach's sign-off on anything long | The coach |
| **8. Make it the plan** | My Plan | Direction, course, funding and placement written into the person's plan, with one light job action a week while studying | Progress Support |

A person can enter at any step. Someone who already knows their direction goes straight to step 4; someone with a gap from a real advert arrives at step 4 from Match Analysis; someone with no target starts at step 1.

### The course-fit engine

The engine was planned in June 2026, and its data sources were verified as open and live at the same time. It does one thing well: given a gap or a direction, it finds the real courses that close it, and ranks them honestly.

| Stage | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Read the gap** | Takes the gaps from Match Analysis or Plan Analysis, or the chosen direction | The person's case |
| **Translate to skills** | Maps each gap onto the national taxonomy of occupations and skills | JobTech Taxonomy |
| **Find courses** | Searches every publicly funded course in Sweden: komvux, vocational college, folk high school, university, labour-market training | Susa-navet (Skolverket), daily |
| **Link course to occupation** | Checks which occupations and skills each course actually leads to | JobEd Connect |
| **Weigh demand** | Adds the shortage level and outlook for the occupation, and live advert volume | Yrkesbarometern, SCB, JobTech |
| **Weigh outcomes** | Adds what happened to earlier graduates, year-tagged | SCB and the vocational college authority |
| **Rank** | Gap closed × demand × outcome, with short, free and placement-including courses lifted | Code, not a model |
| **Write the learning plan** | A short list per gap: course, provider, place, start, length, cost, outcome note, recognition shortcut, and "apply now anyway" beside it | Into My Plan and the Case Record |

The engine never invents a course, a recognition or an outcome. Where a data source has no answer, the learning plan says so.

### How it is built

| Step | Delivers | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The course-fit engine and learning plan** | Courses that close real gaps, with the fork always shown | Every other step uses it; it is useful on day one from Match Analysis |
| **2. Transition Compass** | The why, the runway and the routes back from sick leave | Shapes everything after it |
| **3. Funding Navigator** | Eligibility and the application pack, ready before the window | The equity promise; the October and April windows |
| **4. Direction Explorer and Prior Learning Check** | Directions and recognition | Needs the taxonomy layer already built in step 1 |
| **5. Transition Economics and Test Before You Invest** | Months and money; trying before investing | Builds on the earlier steps |

The Omställning Guide is therefore not a new idea waiting for a plan. It is the Education Matcher's engine, wrapped in a guided journey that starts with why, ends with a plan, and keeps the coach and the fork in it all the way.

## 8. Honest Principles for Omställning

- **Never sell escape.** The promise is a better-matched, more durable transition, not a guaranteed way out of a changing labour market.
- **Ask why first.** The reason for the change decides the route and the expectation.
- **Show the fork every time.** Applying now and a placement are always beside re-training.
- **Short and demand-aligned first.** Courses are recommended from demand, not from the catalogue.
- **Recognise before re-training.**
- **Dated facts only.** Every amount, age and date is shown with its year and source, and checked again every year.
- **Make the underserved the best prepared.**
- **Plain Swedish and no digital gate.** Spoken mode and the coach beside the person on every flow.
- **The coach signs off the big decisions.**
