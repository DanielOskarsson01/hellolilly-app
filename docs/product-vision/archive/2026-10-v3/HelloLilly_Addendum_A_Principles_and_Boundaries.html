Section 01

The three load-bearing principles

01

The strategy paper describes the problem and the solution. This addendum holds the rules the solution is built under: the principles that shape every tool, and the things the system will not do. Section 7 of the paper names the three load-bearing principles and points here for the rest.

Principle What it means in practice
The person decides what is true No line, bullet point or figure reaches a CV or letter unless it comes from something the person has confirmed or written themselves. What the person types is saved as fact without friction. A model's suggestion becomes a fact only when the person accepts it, and a fixed check stops an invented line from slipping in on a careless click. Applications may sell and present real experience at its strongest; they may never invent.
The writer never checks its own work The agent or call that drafts is never the one that checks. Every judgement the person relies on (a verdict, a fit claim, a final check) has a separate checker.
Argue for the person, never judge them Present real experience at its maximum truthful strength. When something falls short, the fault lies in the document, the gap or the market, never in the person's character. A hard market truth is stated as a mechanism ("adverts in this field increasingly ask for a certificate you do not hold"), never as a verdict on the person.
Section 02

How decisions are made

02
  • Agents prepare, the person decides. Search, research, drafting and checking happen in the background. The person holds every gate.
  • Nothing is sent in anyone's name. No message, application, post or submission leaves the system without the person pressing send.
  • Suggestions, not actions. The assistant Lilly navigates, explains and drafts handoffs. She never writes or acts on her own, and never re-argues another tool's verdict.
  • Every cut can be overruled. Anything the system filters out carries its reason and an overrule.
  • Scores explain, never judge. A score sorts a long list and always comes with its reasons. It is never presented as an employer's verdict on the person.
  • Block only on truth. The final check stops an application only when a line is not in the person's confirmed bank. Everything else is advice.
  • Human judgement on the biggest decisions. Any plan built on long education needs a coach's sign-off.
  • Augment, never replace. The coach stays the anchor of the relationship.
Section 03

Data, privacy and law

03
  • Consent first. Any recording or transcription happens only with clear, informed consent, recorded before anything is processed. A person can decline without losing support.
  • A legal review before real use. No real person beyond the pilot uses tools that interpret a person's situation until a review of GDPR, the EU AI Act, data controls, the Arbetsförmedlingen framework, incident handling and accessibility has been signed off by a qualified EU-law professional. Until then, no claim of compliance is made.
  • See what it knows, and delete me. Both work before the first real user.
  • Retrieval, not training. The system draws on a living knowledge base that cites its sources. Personal data is never used to train a model.
  • Real-derived is real. Pseudonymised or combined data from real people is treated as real data, never as synthetic.
  • No figure about fewer than five people. Any aggregate shown to anyone other than the person describes at least five people.
  • No consequential decisions. Nothing the system infers about a person ever feeds a decision about their benefits, sanctions, eligibility or rationing.
  • Networks stay with their owners. A coach's contacts stay on the coach's own computer, encrypted. Only a yes or no about a match ever leaves it. A jobseeker's network map is built from their own exports and belongs to them.
  • Public information only. When the system finds people or companies, it uses public information only. Nothing behind a login is ever scraped or automated.
  • Clear sharing boundaries. Public guides, coach-approved resources, consented anonymised examples, private case data and coach-only discussions are kept apart.
  • Support, not surveillance. Activity is logged to help the person remember, restart and recover. Any item can be hidden. It is not a monitoring tool.
Section 04

Honesty in what the system shows

04
  • Honest labels. A screen with sample data says so; an idea not yet built is shown as a concept. A capability that will never exist gets no picture at all.
  • Only real help. The Help right now panel shows a resource only when it actually exists.
  • Honest scope. The Application Check is a simulation of how an application may be read, not access to an employer's own system.
  • A fork, not a funnel. Where re-training is raised, applying directly is always shown beside it. Course outcomes and funding are presented as dated facts, never as proof that a course caused a result, and every such claim traces to a source.
  • Reach the underserved on purpose. The system is built to get the people most exposed to change ready, not only to serve the quick and the confident.
  • No engagement tricks. No streaks, no nagging, no "just checking in".
Section 05

How it reaches real people

05
  • A pilot coach before a vulnerable user. A professional user tests the system on sample cases before the first real jobseeker arrives.
  • No accidental pilots. "Just try it on your CV" at a demo counts as real use and is not allowed before the gates are passed.
  • Demonstrations use sample data only.
Section 06

What we will not build

06

These are deliberate refusals, not postponements.

We will not Why
Send, post or submit anything in the person's name The person stays in charge of what is said about them
Mass outreach to employers Fewer, better attempts; generic outreach harms the person's reputation
Post to LinkedIn through software, or use LinkedIn's API to act for the person The person sends; the tool stops at the send button
Scrape anything behind a login, or automate a person's social accounts Legal and platform risk, and trust
Submit CVs to staffing agencies automatically Assisted only; the person sends
Invent a fact, figure or skill about a person The person decides what is true
Generate a face, or change how a person looks Same face, better photograph
Write in a coach's voice A coach's words are their own
Show invented analytics as if they were real Honest labels
Show poll results before the person has voted Results should not steer answers
Show a "your messages landed well" track record Silence would read as failure
Store interview call audio Privacy
Use inferences about a person in decisions about benefits or eligibility No consequential decisions
Use personal data to train models Retrieval, not training
Let anyone search a coach's network freely Contacts stay with their owner
Read a person's emotions, or interrupt them unprompted Quiet by default
Section 07

What we will not build first

07

These earn their place only once the foundation exists.

  • The full Outcome Engine
  • Advanced Live Support beyond a simple session summary
  • Paid market-signal data (tenders, investments, leadership changes) in Job Radar
  • Separate native iOS and Android apps (an installable web app is enough to start)
  • Fine-tuned models
  • Heavy data integrations beyond public sources
  • A self-built community platform before the decision between hosting and building has been made
  • Push notifications, before in-app reminders prove their value
