## What was deliberately NOT added

- **No TOC change** — nothing is bolted on, so there is nothing extra to list.
- **No raw numbers** — no effect sizes, odds ratios, SEK amounts, percentages or week-counts from the research. Those live in the evidence brief and the funding-rules file, behind the honesty gate.
- **No validering tool** — absent from the consolidated brief; mentioned nowhere.
- **No claims that courses fix hiring bias** — the age/gender finding surfaces only as coach prompts (demand-side strategy) in Transition View.
- **No separate "area" framing** — the words "new area" appear nowhere in the paper. It is simply part of the vision.
