# Knowledge Hub - seed list v1 (~28 real resources)
**Status:** curation candidates for the Hub's first shelf, 2026-07-09. All items are real, public resources - at this size the shelf is both big enough to demo well and honest by default.
**How to use:** a seeding session (Code or Daniel) verifies each link at seed time - institutions are stable but URLs move; verify, do not trust this list's memory. Each item enters the `resources` shape. Approval flow: per D9, **"Daniel (pilotcoach)" is the approver**, which makes the Hub's coach-approval mechanism real on day one rather than fixture.
**Per item below:** name · type · language · why it earns a slot.

---

## CV och personligt brev

1. **Arbetsförmedlingen: Skriva CV** · guide · sv · The official baseline; safe, current, and what a coach would hand over first.
2. **Arbetsförmedlingen: Skriva personligt brev** · guide · sv · Same authority for the letter; pairs with the Cover Letter Builder.
3. **STAR-metoden förklarad** (pick the best sv explainer; Indeed's Swedish career guide has one) · guide · sv · The single most-cited answer structure; feeds Interview Trainer's feedback vocabulary.
4. **Indeed Career Guide: ATS and CV keywords** · guide · en · Honest, concrete take on keyword screening - matches Ansökningskoll's own honesty stance (a fit check, not ATS access).
5. **Europass CV** · tool · sv/en · The EU-standard CV format; relevant for public-sector and EU-wide applications.

## Intervju

6. **Arbetsförmedlingen: Inför anställningsintervjun** · guide · sv · Official interview prep baseline.
7. **Google Interview Warmup** · tool · en · Free spoken practice with instant transcription - the closest public thing to the Interview Trainer, good as a taster.
8. **Yoodli (free tier)** · tool · en · Speech feedback on filler words and pacing; complements rather than competes.
9. **"Vanligaste intervjufrågorna" med svarsexempel** (best sv listing, e.g. Monster.se) · guide · sv · The list everyone googles anyway - better curated than found.
10. **Amy Cuddy: Your body language may shape who you are** · video (TED) · en (sv subs) · The classic confidence-before-the-interview talk; motivational slot material.

## LinkedIn och nätverk

11. **LinkedIn: Profile checklist (official)** · guide · en · The source of truth for profile completeness; feeds LinkedIn Helper.
12. **Så optimerar du din LinkedIn-profil** (best sv guide, e.g. a union's career pages) · guide · sv · The Swedish-market angle: photo norms, headline conventions, sv/en profile choice.
13. **Informational interviews - how to ask** (Indeed or HBR) · guide · en · The mechanism behind Outreach Plan's "ask for advice, not jobs" rule.
14. **Unionen: karriärtjänster** · service · sv · Free CV review and career coaching for members - a real human-help pointer, which the Hub should always carry.

## Söka jobb och läsa marknaden

18. **SACO: Lönesök** · tool · sv · Salary reality per profession; feeds the "salary reality" slot in direction conversations.
19. **Lönestatistik från SCB (lönesök)** · tool · sv · The neutral statistical source beside SACO's member data.

## Omställning och utbildning

## Grupper och barriärer

25. **Jobbsprånget** · programme · sv/en · Internships for newcomer academics - a real route, not advice.

## Curation rules applied (and to keep applying)

- Official and durable beats clever and fragile: Arbetsförmedlingen, CSN, Skolverket, 1177 age well; listicles rot.
- Every category carries at least one *tool or human service*, not only reading - the Hub's promise is help, not homework.
- Mixed sv/en is fine per the language programme; sv is the default, en items earn their place.
- Nothing paywalled except member services clearly labelled as such (Unionen).
- Items 3, 9, 12 need a "pick the best current one" judgement at seed time - the slot is decided, the winner is verified then.
