## DECISION 1 (Daniel, 2026-07-20): the claim unit - free-form generation, semantic safety is DISCIPLINE

Both reviewers proved the fabrication invariant, as written, cannot be a deterministic INVARIANT. The break is **claims made by deletion, not addition**: input "Jag hjälpte teamledaren ... men jag ledde inte teamet" -> output "Jag ledde ett team" introduces no new token yet inverts a denial into a claim. A literal token-checker cannot catch this; a model-judge that can is not deterministic. Two roads were put to Daniel: (A) free-form generation with semantic no-fabrication reclassified as a zero-tolerance DISCIPLINE; (B) typed claim atoms rendered through deterministic templates, preserving a true INVARIANT.

**Daniel chose A.**

Consequences, binding on the brief:
- **The brief's Section 4 and Section 6 are amended:** "Nothing enters the CV that the user has not confirmed" is retained as the product goal and as a **zero-tolerance DISCIPLINE** (judged, human-authored regression corpus, no silent regression), NOT as a CI-provable INVARIANT. Every place the brief calls semantic no-fabrication an INVARIANT is corrected to DISCIPLINE. This is the honesty the vocabulary exists to enforce (D12).
- **What REMAINS a genuine INVARIANT** (deterministic, CI-enforced), stated so the line is clear: (i) every claim-bearing node in an exported CV resolves to a datafact that is `status: verified` or to an exact user-authored assertion - provenance-graph closure; (ii) `unverified` facts are excluded from generation and export; (iii) exact-revision identity - the bytes approved are the bytes stored and exported; (iv) server-only model access, envelope-on-all-untrusted-input, schema-validation-fails-closed. These are provable and stay INVARIANT.
- **The honest external promise** becomes: "the AI is held to never introducing biography you did not supply, checked by an independent model and tested against an adversarial corpus that includes deletion, negation, aggregation and agency attacks - fabrication is caught and regression-gated, not merely discouraged." NOT "fabrication is structurally impossible." The strategy paper's factual-safety claim is updated to this wording before it is shown to anyone; "level 3-4, structurally impossible" is retired in favour of the defensible claim.
- **Because A is a DISCIPLINE, the checker and corpus carry the weight** - so DECISION 3 and the amendments on the checker, the three-event separation, and the eval oracle below are not optional polish; they are what makes A safe enough to ship. A chosen without them would be the editable-box-plus-disclaimer the brief rightly disdains.

## DECISION 2 (Daniel, 2026-07-20, correcting the planner): no route collision - builder fills the reserved slot

The planner initially flagged a route fight over the `#cv` screen. Daniel corrected the record: Wave 1 already renamed the job-bound tailor to **Anpassad CV** at `#cv` (its honest name - it selects and reorders approved lines against one advert; it does not build). **CV-byggaren** is already a reserved, honestly-disabled "Kommer" menu item with nothing behind it. So there is no collision and nothing is deleted; D17's two-surface world already exists in the menu.

What survives as a **binding brief amendment** (not a choice - a seam-integrity requirement, from Codex Finding 6):
- The new builder fills the reserved CV-byggaren slot with its **own route and its own submodules**, distinct from `#cv`/Anpassad CV.
- The builder **never reads an active application case** (advert, decoded role, fit score) and **never invokes the cover-letter writer**. The `#cv` generate path today runs both the tailor and the writer; the builder's path must do neither.
- **A test asserts the isolation**: the builder starts with no active case, reads no advert/role/fit, and never calls the writer. CI-enforced, not merely intended.
- D23's text explicitly preserves Anpassad CV unchanged and does not retire D17. No silent deletion of a shipped tool.

## DECISION 3 (adopted from both reviewers): legacy reconciliation replaces the compat rule we had picked

Two turns before the review, Daniel and the planner selected "verified-legacy at accessor" (missing `status` reads as `verified`) to protect the live store from reseed id-drift. **The review overturns this** (Gemini Axis 6 BLOCKER, Codex Finding 4 BLOCKER, convergent). Codex verified against live data: the store holds 144 datafacts, all lacking `status`; eleven are `type: "fill-gap"` - model-authored bullets minted by `bullet-judge.cjs` without Daniel confirming the exact wording. A blanket verified-default would certify those eleven fabrications as guaranteed-safe the moment slice 1 ships. The opposite blanket (default-unverified) poisons nothing but forces all 144 through review UI and still mis-handles the mapper-concatenated prose facts.

**Neither blanket. The binding fix (a brief amendment, gating slice 1):**
- A **mandatory, ID-preserving, pre-slice-1 reconciliation pass** (a targeted repair, NOT a general migration framework - the brief's no-migration-machinery rule stands):
  - Inventory every legacy datafact by origin (mapper-seeded from `ingest-cv.cjs`, model-written via fill-gap, directly answered, unknown).
  - Backfill `status: verified` **only** where a row traces to a known, hashed, explicitly attested source.
  - **Quarantine as `unverified`** every `fill-gap` row, every derived-prose row (`professional_summary`, `identity_positioning`, `value_proposition`, `star_story`, `leadership`, and any concatenated-field fact), and every unknown-origin row - for Daniel's explicit confirm-or-delete.
  - IDs preserved throughout (no wipe-reseed; no id-drift; no dangling refs).
- **The accessor default is now `unverified`** for any row that somehow still lacks a status after reconciliation - fail safe, not fail permissive. The `verifiedOnly` filter lives at the single accessor chokepoint (unchanged from our prior decision on where it lives).
- **Test:** an upgrade fixture containing mapper facts, model-written facts, and existing refs, run twice for idempotency, stable IDs, complete provenance, no dangling refs, and zero generator visibility of quarantined rows.
- This supersedes the "verified-legacy at accessor" instruction given to the CV-builder session on 2026-07-19. That instruction is void; this reconciliation replaces it. (The Slice 0 report already committed on docs-cv-byggaren-slice0 records the old rule - a follow-up note on that branch, or in the D23 commit, marks it superseded by this record.)

## BINDING BRIEF AMENDMENTS (from convergent + adopted BLOCKER findings)

These fold into the brief before slice 1. Each cites its finding(s).

**A. Three-event separation on claim acceptance** (Gemini Axis 1, Codex Finding 2, convergent BLOCKER). The bullet contract's "flag the claim-diff, user confirms, mint verified fact" launders AI invention into evidence: propose "Prisbelönt säljare" from input "Säljare", user clicks the blanket "Ja", an AI-authored claim becomes a verified datafact. Fix: the brief must separate three events that it currently conflates - (1) a **source assertion** of an atomic fact (from the user or a document), (2) **semantic validation** of a derivation against sources, (3) **approval of final wording**. An unsupported factual diff is **rejected, never cured inline**. If it might be true, the tool asks a neutral question and mints the *answer* as a new user-authored fact, then regenerates. Wording approval may approve presentation; it may never manufacture evidence.

**B. The checker is a semantic judge, not a token-matcher, and is therefore DISCIPLINE** (Gemini Axis 1 MAJOR, Codex Finding 1, convergent). A literal token checker floods the user with false positives on grammar (every connective and inflected word highlighted), training rubber-stamping - and still misses the deletion attack. The checker contract is rewritten: it checks for **semantic claim additions** (new entities, numbers, seniority, causality, agency, polarity, modality, quantity, referent, frequency, tenure) against the cited source facts. Being a model judgment, it is DISCIPLINE per DECISION 1. The maker/checker independence rule stands (a generator self-grading is still not a checker).

**C. Aggregation and transitive taint** (Gemini Axis 1 MAJOR + Codex Finding 12 BLOCKER). Two related leaks: (i) individually-sourced summary sentences combining into an unmade claim - "Projektledare 2019" + "Projektledare 2023" -> "fem års erfarenhet"; (ii) generated prose becoming independent "evidence" on the next pass - a composed "Ledde ett team" (from an input where Sara led) becomes the summary's "dokumenterad ledarerfarenhet" four hops later. Fix: (1) the summary/skill prompts explicitly forbid temporal and quantitative aggregation across distinct facts; (2) provenance is a **DAG closed to immutable user/source atoms** - generated prose never becomes independent evidence, every derived node's citations resolve back to leaf user/source facts; (3) the **host, not the maker, supplies the complete relevant fact set** including negative answers and contradictions ("ingen personalansvar"), so the maker cannot cite a selectively favourable subset. Aggregation and transitive-taint cases join the eval corpus.

**D. Extraction context-inversion and verbatim-span-is-not-evidence** (Gemini Axis 4 MAJOR, Codex Finding 10 MAJOR, convergent). A verbatim span under the header "Arbetsuppgifter jag absolut vill undvika:" gets extracted as an experience the user then rubber-stamps; a two-column OCR misread binds a duty to the wrong role. Fix: (1) "structural-risk detection" in slice 3 is specified concretely to include **hierarchical context mapping** (extract and carry the parent header/section) and column/reading-order hazards, not left as a hopeful label; (2) the source object becomes a **discriminated union** with an immutable asset/text-revision hash, page and layout-block identity, declared offset units, bounds, and quote hash - a schema-valid `{origin, ref?, span?}` with optional fields is not evidence; (3) high-risk fields (dates, numbers, role-duty edges) are confirmed atomically with the original page crop and surrounding heading shown; (4) a user correction **preserves the extraction source and adds a separate exact user-correction event** - it never rewrites the span so the citation lies.

**E. Exact-revision binding: confirmation covers the bytes stored and exported** (Codex Finding 3 BLOCKER + Finding 15 MAJOR). The wireframe shows summary A, writes truncated B; education/skills buttons write hard-coded content independent of the visible inputs. Approval attaches to the wrong revision. Fix: define the **canonical claim/document model before slice 2** (the brief currently introduces it in slice 5 - that is retrofit, not born-compliance). Every claim-bearing node has immutable text or typed atoms, pinned source-fact revisions, and a **confirmation receipt over the exact content hash**. `accept` submits an opaque candidate ID + revision hash; the server retrieves the candidate actually shown. Any edit, rewrite, easy-Swedish variant, translation, regeneration, or source change creates a **new pending revision** and invalidates prior checker/confirmation attestations. Facts are **immutable revisions - corrections append and supersede, never overwrite in place** (the current store upserts by ID; that is the bug). Preview, saved versions, plain-text view, and PDF all render that same pinned revision.

**F. The version container is host-owned, never a raw collections write** (Codex Finding 5 BLOCKER). The brief's "lighter default" - snapshots in the client-writable collections store - is a direct ledger bypass: a client can POST a snapshot with arbitrary claim text (`POST /api/collection/:name` is ungated, `putRecord()` is ungated, any `store`-capability submodule can write any collection). Fix: named versions live in a **host-owned canonical-version store or a reserved namespace writable only through a version-specific server API**. Client state carries ordering, presentation, and **pinned verified revision IDs - never authoritative claim text**. The server resolves all rendered text and rejects client-supplied claim strings, mismatched ref/text pairs, dangling refs, and unverified refs. This resolves the brief's "version container" open decision (Section 9) in favour of the host-owned option; the collections-snapshot default is withdrawn.

**G. The candidate-review reader seam** (Codex Finding 11 BLOCKER). "Unverified is excluded from every reader" (slice 1) contradicts "the review UI reads those candidates" (slice 3). Filtering the sole accessor hides the review inbox; leaving a raw reader exposed is a generator bypass; and the existing writing-gate exemption (`collectRefdFactTexts()` exempts any referenced datafact text without checking verification state) is a second bypass a direct known-ID reference can exploit. Fix: two capabilities - an **ingestion-batch-scoped candidate API available only to the host review flow**, and a **verified-only evidence API** used by every generator, checker, renderer, citation exemption, and exporter. The citation exemption is corrected to check verification state. Define the candidate lifecycle `pending -> accepted/rejected/superseded`; resolved candidates never count as pending (so an ignored upload cannot block export forever); specify whether acceptance transitions the object or mints a separate verified fact (pick one, state it).

**H. The export gate: one predicate, fail closed, not called an INVARIANT-by-construction** (Gemini Axis 5 MINOR, Codex Finding 17 MAJOR, convergent on circularity). Fix: state **one server-side predicate** - every claim-bearing leaf in the exact immutable export graph resolves to an allowed `verified` source revision or an exact user-authored assertion, and the rendered claim is the accepted revision; missing or mismatched provenance **fails closed**; only enumerated structural text (headings, punctuation) is exempt. Pending candidates outside the graph are a specified warning or blocker, not an infinite hang. The UI checklist is **computed from that predicate** and rerun on the exact bytes rendered to the downloadable artifact (the wireframe's hard-coded-green checks are a demonstration bug - see amendment J). The gate enforces provenance-closure (a real INVARIANT); it does not enforce semantic truth (that is the checker's DISCIPLINE) - the brief stops conflating the two.

**I. The eval corpus: a sound oracle and a complete-before-merge gate** (Gemini Axis 3, Codex Finding 8 BLOCKER, convergent). The brief never defines CI's pass/fail predicate and says the corpus "begins" in slice 2 even though the composer merges there - a merge gate cannot be partly present. Fix: (1) per fixture, **hand-authored allowed and forbidden atomic claims**, validated against the typed/parsed output **independently of the production checker** (reusing the checker as its own oracle is circular); (2) **mutation tests** - every mutant must fail: delete a negation, swap agents, swap quantities, attach a duty to another role, invent causality, upgrade assistance to ownership, remove uncertainty, translate a credential upward, reuse a rejected/unverified fact; (3) **metamorphic laws** - "adding `inte` invalidates the positive claim", "moving a duty moves every derivative"; (4) freeze the corpus, normalization, oracle, prompt/checker config, and model-change policy (the frozen zone, per D12 Rule 4); (5) **the complete slice-2 corpus gates the slice-2 merge**; slice 3 extends it before the extraction/voice merge. Because DECISION 1 makes semantic safety a DISCIPLINE, this corpus is the primary evidence that the DISCIPLINE holds - it is load-bearing, not supplementary.

**J. Correct the wireframe before it is treated as canonical** (Codex Finding 9 MAJOR). The approved "what" spec exports fabrications today: the summary literal asserts "tolv års erfarenhet" and "hålla lugnet" with no supporting input; the skills button writes "Truckkort A+B" whether or not a licence was uploaded; Step 6 is reachable directly with all safety checks hard-coded green and download enabled. A faithful implementation reproduces the exact harm the brief forbids. Fix: correct `cv-byggaren-wireframe-v2.html` before it is canonical - every displayed clause traces to a shown input; suggestions render as explicit pending tri-state objects; remove all hard-coded claim insertion; Step 6 gains real blocked/error/review states. Acceptance test: from a fresh store, clicking every forward/accept/download control without entering or confirming anything yields **zero CV claims and no export**. (This is a planner/design task on the wireframe artifact, sequenced before slice 2 build; the wireframe is this thread's to correct.)

**K. Uncontracted generation paths: translation, easy-Swedish, manual edit, voice** (Codex Findings 13 and 14 MAJOR). Step 6 offers Svenska/English but there is no translation contract - "biträdde teamledaren" can become "Assistant Team Leader responsible for scheduling", "påbörjad utbildning, ingen examen" can become "Bachelor's degree", and the structural gate passes the strengthened English claim. Easy-Swedish rewrite and manual edit have the same invalidation gap. Voice is shown in slice 2 (guided field dictation) but architected in slice 3 (bulk transcript extraction) - different truth semantics, and dropped ASR negation ("inte") mints a false verified answer either way. Fix: (1) either **remove English CV output from this build** or add a translation contract with independent semantic check, variant-specific provenance, exact-revision acceptance, and adversarial evals; (2) every easy-Swedish rewrite creates a new pending revision and reruns the checks; (3) a manual edit is user-authored material that **creates a new exact user-source event and invalidates all prior attestations**; (4) **decide before slice 2 whether voice is field dictation or bulk narration**, disable the voice door until its slice exists, and in either case bind to an immutable finalized transcript revision with correction/confirmation (never a mutable interim ASR result).

**L. Category reseed-safety needs a reproducible source and stable IDs** (Codex Finding 16 MAJOR). Twenty-five live competency facts carry a `category` object sourced from `COMPETENCY_MASTER_POOL.json`; canonical `data/cv_data.json` has no category field and the mapper never produces one; the real-data mapper test is skipped in a clean checkout because the personal file is gitignored. Fix: put the **non-personal category taxonomy/mapping in a versioned canonical source** (or add stable category keys to the input format); define **deterministic source-derived IDs or an atomic old->new ref remap**; add a **committed synthetic fixture with the exact production shape** and compare full structural equality (IDs, text, status, sources, categories, refs) across two rebuilds. Do not rely on the gitignored real CV to make a CI invariant executable. (Ties to DECISION 3's reconciliation and to the twin-persona fixture already committed.)
