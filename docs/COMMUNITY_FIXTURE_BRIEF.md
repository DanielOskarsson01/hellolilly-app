## 5. Content rules for the writing session

- Swedish throughout; casual register, real typos NOT simulated (fixture, not theatre).
- Thread lengths 4-8 posts; no thread ends in a miracle; wins are small and specific.
- Coach posts are rarer than peer posts and never lecture - one useful line, the paper's "motivate, advise, uplift" tone.
- Anti-blame is hard law in every post, including peer voices: mechanics, never traits, even in self-description ("mina ansökningar träffar fel" over "jag är dålig på att söka").
- Nothing medical, nothing benefits-legal, nothing about named real employers in negative terms.
- Dates relative ("för två veckor sedan"), so fixtures never rot.
